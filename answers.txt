Question 2:

Scrum Master: The scrum master is in charge of carrying out the product owner’s decisions, and making sure the product produced by the team is up to standards. They ensure the team is meeting deadlines and working hard.

Product Owner: The product owner is the one who controls the overall output of the project. They develop and convey the overall project goal, and make the decisions that influence how the project will be developed.
